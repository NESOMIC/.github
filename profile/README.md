# Nesomic

Nesomic (nesoi | omics) gives scientific support to decision making for biodiversity conservation. Research teams manage and analyse DNA sequences, phylogenetic trees and genomes together with geographic layers and maps.

Nesomic is an initiative promoted by the Jardín Botánico Canario "Viera y Clavijo" - Unidad Asociada al CSIC, and developed at the Instituto Tecnológico de Canarias (ITC). It was born in the NEXTGENDEM project.
