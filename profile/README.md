# Nesomic

Nesomic (nesoi | omics) gives scientific support to decision making for biodiversity conservation. Research teams manage and analyse DNA sequences, phylogenetic trees and genomes together with geographic layers and maps. Born in the NEXTGENDEM project, developed at the Instituto Tecnológico de Canarias (ITC).
